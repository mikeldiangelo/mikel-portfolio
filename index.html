Personal Portfolio

Designed and built to convert visitors

--Challange

As a freelancer, having no online presence means relying entirely on word of mouth to get clients. There was no central place to showcase skills, or list services. You can put yourself out there through social media but a solid foundation for assurance needs to be set.

--Execution

Built a clean multi-section portfolio using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5 and vanilla javascript. Sections lnclude hero, about, skills with animated progress bars, service, projects showcase with filter tabs and a contact form. The form validates inputs and redirects directly to Whatsapp and also email, incase of online activity for easier responce while keeping communication fast.

--Result

A fully mobile-responsive and deployed portfolio that sreves as a 24/7 sales page. Clients can view work, understand services offered and reach out in under a minute without needing to search elsewhere.

Key Achievement

Reduced client onboarding friction by connecting the contact form directly to Whatsapp, cutting response time significantly compared to traditional email forms

Cloud Hosting Website

A Demo Cloud Hosting Platform

--Challange

Many hosting platforms have cluttered, overwhelming landing pages that confuse potential customers before they even sign up. The goal was to design a clean, modern front-end that communicates value immediately and guides visitors toward taking action.

--Execution

Designed and built the Loruki cloud hosting website using HTML and CSS. The layout includes a hero section with a clear value proposition, a features breakdown, a pricing section and a demo request form. The design prioritises clarity and visual hierarchy to move visitors down the page naturally.

--Result

A fully responsive product marketing site that presents a hosting platform in a trustworthy and professional light. The demo request form gives the business a direct lead generation tool without needing a backend at the front-end stage.

Key Achievement

Delivered a clean, conversion focused layout that balances product information with strong call-to-action placement across every section of the page

Social Media Campaign

Full content strategy and execution for an entertainment page

--Challange

The page had an existing social media presence but lacked consistency in posting, visual identity and messaging. Low engagement and inconsistent content were making it difficult to build an audience and drive visual awareness.

--Execution

Designed a full content strategy for their Facebook page. Created branded graphics using Canva, wrote captions tailored to their platform's audience, developed a monthly content calendar and ran targeted Meta Ads to expand reach beyond the existing follower base.

--Result

Engagement grew by 40% within the campaign period. The page's feed became visually consistent, post reach increased through paid promotion and the content calendar ensured the page stayed active without gaps.

Key Achievement

Transformed an inconsistent social presence into a structured content system, resulting in a 40% engagement increase and a recognisable brand identity on the facebook platform.
